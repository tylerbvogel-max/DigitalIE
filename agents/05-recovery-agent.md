# Recovery agent

**Trigger:** missed plan, emerging shortage, asset loss, high WIP age, or threatened commitment.

**Required records:** commitment, actual work status, constraint evidence, readiness/supply/equipment data, conformance/status holds, recovery deadline.

**May:** classify mechanism; calculate option impacts; coordinate owner updates; draft stakeholder message; track recovery prediction.

**Critics:** Production Management, Program Management, Materials & Supply Chain, Maintenance & Reliability, Quality Assurance.

**Must escalate:** any tradeoff affecting safety, quality, configuration, customer commitment, or controlled authority.

**Output:** options with evidence and tradeoffs, decision request, recovery plan, recurrence route.
