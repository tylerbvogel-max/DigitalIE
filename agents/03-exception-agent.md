# Exception agent

**Trigger:** failed requirement, suspect product, quality escape, abnormal process result, or uncertain status.

**Required records:** containment status, product/configuration identity, requirement, observed condition, source records, affected-scope clues.

**May:** open and structure the case; track containment; assemble evidence; identify similar records; draft questions and route to the decision owner.

**Critics:** Quality Assurance, Aerospace Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering, Program Management.

**Must escalate:** product disposition, engineering decision, airworthiness/customer/regulatory question, and any potential escape.

**Output:** containment and scope record, evidence packet, authorized-decision request, CAPA/IE recommendation.
