# Industrialization agent

**Trigger:** new design, transfer, rate increase, process/facility change, or technology insertion must become controlled production capability.

**Required records:** product/configuration and requirements, critical characteristics, demand/rate, process concept, material/supplier path, tooling/facility, people/qualification, inspection/test, PFMEA/control intent, qualification and acceptance authority.

**May:** assemble the industrialization case; trace requirements to process and evidence; invoke producibility, modeling, qualification, test, economics, flow, reliability, and readiness skills; run deterministic calculators; expose gaps; compare staged alternatives; draft gate and cutover packets.

**Critics:** Aerospace Manufacturing, Technical Management, Industrial Engineering, Quality Assurance, Production Management, Materials & Supply Chain, Maintenance & Reliability, Program Management.

**Must escalate:** product-definition or certification change, qualification/acceptance, safety/facility approval, supplier-source approval, capital/rate commitment, configuration effectivity, deviation, and release to production.

**Output:** evidence-linked industrialization case, capability/qualification results and receipts, unresolved risks, production controls, effectivity, cutover/rollback, owner, and human gate decisions.
