# Design to production capability

**Trigger:** new design, product transition, process/facility transfer, rate increase, manufacturing technology insertion, or material/process change.

1. Establish product/configuration, requirements, critical characteristics, demand/rate, acceptance basis, and industrialization authority.
2. Translate the design into material, process, tooling, facility, people, supplier, inspection, test, data, and sustainment needs.
3. Challenge producibility and failure modes with representative manufacturing, quality, supply, maintenance, safety, and operator evidence.
4. Plan qualification and verification within an explicit application envelope; preserve raw evidence and anomalies.
5. Demonstrate representative capability and flow, resolve or accept gaps through authorized decisions, and define production controls.
6. Control effectivity, cutover, WIP, monitoring, degraded mode, rollback, and ownership through release and scale.

**Outputs:** industrialization case, requirement-to-process/evidence map, capability and readiness results, qualification package, risks, cutover plan, production controls, and gate decisions.

**Calls:** Aerospace Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering, Quality Assurance, Technical Management, Production Management, Materials & Supply Chain, Maintenance & Reliability, and Program Management.

## Process contract

| State | Minimum evidence | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| Framed | approved product/configuration boundary, requirements, demand, acceptance, owner | industrialization scope and gate authority recorded |
| Designed | process architecture, producibility review, dependencies, PFMEA/control intent, qualification strategy | alternatives and unresolved gaps visible |
| Demonstrated | representative article/process, source data, receipts, anomalies, capacity/flow and acceptance evidence | evidence supports the declared application envelope or gaps are held |
| Decision pending | readiness, residual risk, configuration/effectivity, cutover/rollback, ownership | authorized release/hold/rework decision recorded |
| Released and monitored | effective definition, trained/qualified resources, production controls, reaction plan | sustained measures meet criteria or trigger governed recovery/change |

**Decision gates:** design and configuration authority owns product-definition changes; authorized engineering/quality/customer roles own qualification and acceptance; safety/facilities roles own their controlled boundaries; program/operations authority owns rate and investment commitments.

**Handoffs:** Commitment to ready work receives effective configuration, demonstrated capability, dependencies, residual risks, and release boundary. Change to effective control receives any post-baseline change. Exception to disposition receives affected product and anomaly evidence.

**Measures:** late producibility changes, first-pass yield at launch, qualification escapes, rate-demonstration accuracy, time from design release to stable output, cutover defects, and recurrence of accepted industrialization risks.
