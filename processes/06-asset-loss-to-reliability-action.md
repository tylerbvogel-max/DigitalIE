# Asset loss to reliability action

**Trigger:** breakdown, recurring loss, degraded performance, or maintenance risk on a production asset.

1. Put equipment and affected product into a safe, controlled state.
2. Record asset identity, symptom, production impact, product/status impact, and operating context.
3. Plan and execute qualified repair with parts, safety controls, and return-to-service verification.
4. Classify loss and test for repeat failure, PM weakness, spares risk, or operating-condition cause.
5. Update maintenance strategy, spares, work instructions, or production plan when evidence supports it.

**Outputs:** verified return-to-service, loss record, reliability action, and production recovery input.

**Calls:** Maintenance & Reliability, Production Management, Quality Assurance, Industrial Engineering.
