# Change to effective control

**Trigger:** proposed design, process, tooling, supplier, software, document, or planning change.

1. Define change, authority, objective, and requested effective boundary.
2. Map effect on configuration, WIP/inventory, route/instructions, tooling, qualifications, controls, suppliers, systems, and commitments.
3. Obtain required approval; set serial, lot, order, or configuration cut-in.
4. Disposition affected existing product through the authorized route.
5. Release all updated point-of-use information together; verify training, records, and first effective execution.

**Outputs:** approved change record, impact assessment, cut-in plan, WIP disposition, and effectiveness evidence.

**Calls:** Aerospace Manufacturing, Quality Assurance, Production Management, Program Management, Digital Operations & Data.

## Process contract

| State | Minimum evidence | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| Proposed | objective, change definition, initiator, affected baseline hypothesis | change owner and authority route assigned |
| Impact assessment | configuration, WIP/stock, supplier, tooling, qualification, data, commitment impacts | known impacts, unknowns, verification, rollback/containment defined |
| Approval pending | alternatives, risks, effectivity proposal, resources | authorized decision and conditions recorded |
| Released | approved artifacts and effective boundary | synchronized point-of-use release confirmed |
| Implementing | affected population and execution plan | old/new boundary controlled; WIP/stock disposition recorded |
| Effectiveness | verification and outcome criteria | result and residual risk accepted or change revised/reversed |

**Decision gates:** technical classification, configuration approval, WIP disposition, customer/regulatory notification, process qualification, controlled-data release, and commitment changes use their defined authorities.

**Measures:** approval and implementation lead time, partial-release defects, effectivity ambiguity, WIP discovered after cut-in, first-effective execution success, rollback/containment events, and intended outcome achieved.
