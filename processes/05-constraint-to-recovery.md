# Constraint to recovery

**Trigger:** missed plan, aging WIP, shortage, capacity loss, or threatened commitment.

1. State the missed commitment, actual status, impact, and decision deadline.
2. Identify the governing constraint and evidence: readiness, material, equipment, labor, yield, inspection, supplier, or approval.
3. Generate recovery options with impact on safety, conformance, configuration, cost, and downstream work.
4. Obtain the required authority for the selected tradeoff; do not create uncontrolled expedites.
5. Measure recovery and route recurring mechanisms to IE, QA, reliability, or program risk.

**Outputs:** recovery decision, owner, time-bound actions, stakeholder update, and recurrence path.

**Calls:** Production Management, Program Management, Materials & Supply Chain, Maintenance & Reliability, Quality Assurance.

## Process contract

| State | Minimum evidence | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| Threat detected | commitment, actual, variance, exposure window | recovery owner and decision deadline assigned |
| Constraint verified | flow evidence distinguishing constraint from symptom | governing constraint and uncertainty recorded |
| Options | modeled throughput/date, dependencies, quality/configuration/cost effects | feasible alternatives and no-action consequence presented |
| Decision pending | authority and stakeholder impacts | authorized option, conditions, communication, trigger thresholds recorded |
| Executing | owners, sequence, resources, leading indicators | recovery trajectory confirmed or contingency triggered |
| Stabilized | sustained performance and residual backlog/risk | recurrence routed and temporary controls retired or standardized |

**Guardrail:** expedite priority never authorizes bypass of configuration, product status, qualification, inspection, safety, or record controls.

**Decision gates:** customer/program commitment changes, controlled priority, overtime or capacity expenditure, alternate/substitute use, configuration or process departure, product disposition, and any safety/quality trade require their applicable authorized roles.

**Measures:** time to verified constraint, recovery forecast error, commitment recovery, premium cost, downstream disruption, uncontrolled expedite count, recurrence, and temporary control aging.
