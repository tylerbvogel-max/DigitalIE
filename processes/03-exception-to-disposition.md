# Exception to disposition

**Trigger:** failed requirement, suspect record, quality escape, abnormal process result, or uncertain product status.

1. Make the condition safe and contain physical product, digital status, and related work.
2. Preserve product identity, governing requirement, source records, and observed condition.
3. Scope potentially affected serials, lots, configurations, operations, and delivered product.
4. Assemble an evidence packet and route it to the authorized quality, engineering, airworthiness, or customer decision owner.
5. Implement and verify the authorized disposition; open CAPA/IE work when recurrence or system risk warrants it.

**Outputs:** containment record, scope assessment, authorized disposition, and corrective-action decision.

**Calls:** Quality Assurance, Aerospace Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering, Program Management.
