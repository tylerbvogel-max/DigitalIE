# Exception to disposition

**Trigger:** failed requirement, suspect record, quality escape, abnormal process result, or uncertain product status.

1. Make the condition safe and contain physical product, digital status, and related work.
2. Preserve product identity, governing requirement, source records, and observed condition.
3. Scope potentially affected serials, lots, configurations, operations, and delivered product.
4. Assemble an evidence packet and route it to the authorized quality, engineering, airworthiness, or customer decision owner.
5. Implement and verify the authorized disposition; open CAPA/IE work when recurrence or system risk warrants it.

**Outputs:** containment record, scope assessment, authorized disposition, and corrective-action decision.

**Calls:** Quality Assurance, Aerospace Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering, Program Management.

## Process contract

| State | Minimum evidence | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | observed condition, reporter, time, product/process identity | immediate safety, product, record, and downstream controls assigned |
| Containment | suspect scope hypothesis and status controls | known exposure controlled or uncertainty escalated |
| Scope/evidence | governing requirement, configuration, genealogy, source records | decision packet identifies affected and potentially affected population |
| Disposition pending | facts separated from hypotheses; applicable authority known | authorized disposition and conditions recorded |
| Implementation | affected items, instructions, verification need | disposition completed and independently verified as required |
| Recurrence decision | mechanism/history/risk assessment | CAPA, IE, supplier, change, monitor, or no-further-action route recorded |

**Escalation clock:** local procedure sets times, but potential safety, regulatory, customer, delivered-product, or uncontrolled-scope exposure is escalated immediately. Aging begins at detection, not at formal record creation.

**Decision gates:** product disposition, technical compliance, airworthiness, customer notification, and release remain with their authorized roles.

**Handoffs:** Change receives approved definition/process change need and exposed effectivity; recovery receives commitment impact; CAPA/IE receives recurring mechanism and validation need; build receives authorized disposition and verified status.

**Measures:** containment latency, time to credible scope, disposition aging, repeat condition, escape expansion after initial containment, implementation errors, and effectiveness-review completion.
