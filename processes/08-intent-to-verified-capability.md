# Intent to verified AI capability

**Trigger:** recurring work or a decision may benefit from deterministic automation, AI assistance, an agent, a skill, an MCP portal, or a software-factory pattern.

1. Frame the user, work/decision, current path, outcome, frequency, evidence, error cost, and authority boundary.
2. Select the least complex viable mode: process change, deterministic software, AI-assisted step, or agentic workflow.
3. Define architecture, context, tool/data portal, permissions, human gates, failure behavior, and acceptance evaluation.
4. Build a narrow vertical slice in an isolated environment with representative synthetic or authorized fixtures.
5. Conduct independent code, architecture, security, domain, and operational review; remediate blocking findings.
6. Release through authorized change/deployment control with monitoring, rollback, ownership, and support.
7. Compare operational outcome with baseline; convert failures into regression fixtures and reusable factory assets.

**Outputs:** framed capability case, architecture/context/tool contract, threat model, evaluated implementation, approval packet, release/rollback plan, runtime evidence, and benefits decision.

**Calls:** AI-Native Engineering, Digital Operations & Data, Technical Management, Quality Assurance, and the profile governing the operational decision.

## Process contract

| State | Minimum evidence | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| Opportunity framed | user, work/decision, baseline, volume, error cost, authority | sponsor and measurable outcome confirmed |
| Mode selected | process/deterministic/assisted/agent alternatives | complexity choice and rationale recorded |
| Designed | architecture, context, data/tools, permissions, gates, failure behavior | design/security/domain review ready |
| Built | isolated implementation, versioned configuration, fixtures | deterministic and scenario checks execute reproducibly |
| Evaluation | normal, missing, stale, conflicting, malicious, high-impact cases | thresholds met and residual failures accepted or remediated |
| Release pending | owner, deployment, monitoring, support, rollback, approval | authorized release decision recorded |
| Operated | runtime evidence, overrides, incidents, outcome measure | standardize, revise, scale, restrict, or retire decision |

**Decision gates:** data/system access, security/compliance acceptance, production deployment, controlled-record mutation, manufacturing action, model-risk acceptance, and residual operational risk remain with authorized human roles.

**Handoffs:** software/change management receives the verified release packet; operations receives owner/runbook/monitoring/rollback; governance receives incidents and evaluation gaps; the affected manufacturing process receives only authorized outputs within its decision boundary.

**Measures:** accepted lead time, evaluation escape, change failure, rollback/recovery, human override, harmful/blocked action, rework, reuse, operating cost, adoption-to-action conversion, and measured business outcome.
