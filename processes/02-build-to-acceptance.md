# Build to acceptance

**Trigger:** work is released to the floor or a supplier.

1. Verify current approved configuration, work instructions, material identity/status, tooling, and qualified execution path.
2. Execute the released route while recording as-built evidence at each required operation.
3. Maintain product identity, status, and segregation through handoffs, inspection, test, rework, and storage.
4. Compare results with the governing requirement using a valid method and attributable record.
5. Route accepted product to the authorized release path; route any failure to Exception to disposition.

**Outputs:** complete as-built record, acceptance evidence, authorized status, and traceable handoff.

**Calls:** Aerospace Manufacturing, Production Management, Quality Assurance, Materials & Supply Chain.

## Process contract

| State | Minimum evidence | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| Released | effective product definition, route, material/status, qualified resources | point-of-use readiness verified |
| Executing | product identity and contemporaneous operation records | required operation complete with preserved as-built evidence |
| Verification pending | governing characteristic and valid inspection/test method | attributable result recorded against requirement |
| Acceptance pending | complete requirement-to-evidence chain and exceptions | authorized acceptance/release or exception route recorded |
| Handed off | product/status and record package | receiving function acknowledges custody and status |

**Immediate control:** stop and preserve identity when configuration, material pedigree, required instruction, qualification, equipment status, acceptance evidence, or product status is uncertain.

**Decision gates:** only authorized roles accept or release product, approve departures, or determine compliance. The process may prove completeness; it cannot convert incomplete evidence into conformance.

**Measures:** first-pass acceptance, record-right-first-time, queue and touch time, rework loops, escaped/missing evidence, product-status ambiguity, and release latency.
