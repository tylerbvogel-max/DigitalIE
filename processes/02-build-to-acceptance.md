# Build to acceptance

**Trigger:** work is released to the floor or a supplier.

1. Verify current approved configuration, work instructions, material identity/status, tooling, and qualified execution path.
2. Execute the released route while recording as-built evidence at each required operation.
3. Maintain product identity, status, and segregation through handoffs, inspection, test, rework, and storage.
4. Compare results with the governing requirement using a valid method and attributable record.
5. Route accepted product to the authorized release path; route any failure to Exception to disposition.

**Outputs:** complete as-built record, acceptance evidence, authorized status, and traceable handoff.

**Calls:** Aerospace Manufacturing, Production Management, Quality Assurance, Materials & Supply Chain.
