# Monthly system review

## Purpose

Detect systemic weakness that individual cases and daily management cannot see.

## Review set

- Outcome trends with definitions, denominators, uncertainty, and source lineage
- Repeat mechanisms across exception, downtime, supplier, readiness, and recovery cases
- Aged decisions/actions and authority bottlenecks
- Configuration, evidence, and technical-data integrity signals
- Risk triggers and residual-risk decisions
- Benefits-realization results, including null/adverse interventions
- Local observations, candidate promotions, source currency, and corpus-health failures
- Agent evaluation failures and unsafe recommendation patterns

## Outputs

At most a small number of sponsored system interventions, each with baseline, mechanism, owner, authority, resourcing, review window, and stop/reversal condition. Everything else remains monitored or is explicitly rejected.
