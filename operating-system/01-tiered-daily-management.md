# Tiered daily management

## Purpose

Expose abnormal conditions near the work, resolve them at the lowest authorized level, and escalate only the payload required for the next decision.

## Standard pattern

1. Verify safety and product-control status.
2. Compare actual versus plan for quality, delivery, material, people, and assets.
3. Identify the governing abnormality or constraint; do not read every metric.
4. Assign an owner, next action, decision deadline, and evidence source.
5. Escalate unresolved cross-boundary decisions with affected scope and requested authority.
6. Confirm prior commitments and close only with objective evidence.

Each tier receives a smaller, decision-ready payload rather than a larger status presentation. Local teams retain ownership after escalation unless authority or responsibility is explicitly transferred.

## Measures

Commitment aging, escalation response time, repeat abnormality, overdue action, prediction accuracy, and percentage closed with evidence.
