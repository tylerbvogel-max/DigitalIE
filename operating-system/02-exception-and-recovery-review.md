# Cross-functional exception and recovery review

## Purpose

Resolve aged, high-exposure, or cross-functional cases that cannot be closed within normal flow.

## Required inputs

Affected product/commitment, configuration and status, containment, exposure, authoritative evidence, governing constraint, alternatives, requested decision, owner, and decision deadline.

## Agenda by decision

- New exposure requiring containment or customer/program awareness
- Decision-ready disposition/change/recovery requests
- Cases blocked by missing authority or evidence
- Actions approaching trigger thresholds
- Failed effectiveness reviews and recurrence

Do not use the forum for raw problem discovery, slide narration, or assigning unnamed groups. The chair rejects an item that lacks an explicit decision request unless immediate safety or product control requires intervention.

## Outputs

Recorded decision, conditions, dissent, owner, due date, affected scope, stakeholder communication, and next process state.
