# Daily management system

## Question answered

Can the team recognize abnormality early, make the right decision at the right level, and close the loop without converting every issue into leadership theater?

## Cadence

Use a tiered rhythm suited to the operation: point-of-work huddle; area/cell escalation; cross-functional daily review; and leadership review for systemic/resource decisions. Each tier receives unresolved issues, constraints, and decisions—not a repeated narration of yesterday.

## Standard agenda

Safety; quality/conformance; plan versus actual; constraint/shortage; people/equipment readiness; aging abnormalities; commitments due; and recognition/learning. For each red condition record owner, next action, due time, escalation trigger, and closure evidence.

## Exit

The shift begins with a shared plan and known risks; abnormalities have an owner and route; recurring problems flow into the IE/QA system rather than becoming permanent meeting topics.
