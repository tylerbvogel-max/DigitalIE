# Recovery and escalation

## Question answered

When execution departs from plan, what is the smallest safe response, who decides it, and when does it become a cross-functional or leadership issue?

## Recovery structure

State the event, customer/operational impact, current containment, affected scope, governing constraint, options, quality/configuration/safety checks, decision owner, deadline, predicted outcome, and follow-up measure.

## Escalate when

Authority is unclear; product status/conformance is at risk; a customer commitment needs change; a constraint needs cross-functional resources; a repeated issue requires CAPA/IE work; or the workaround would create hidden risk or record debt.

## Exit

The recovery is authorized, bounded, measured, and either returns work to the normal system or creates a formal improvement case. The team does not normalize emergency work.
