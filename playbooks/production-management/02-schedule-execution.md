# Schedule execution

## Question answered

What should be built now, what can actually be built, and what decision closes the gap without compromising the product?

## Reconcile daily

Compare demand/commitment, released work, configuration-effective plan, actual WIP status, material/tool/people/equipment readiness, expected yield, constraints, inspection/test holds, and downstream capacity. Track reason codes for misses at a useful mechanism level—not merely “late.”

## Recovery rule

Choose a recovery action only after confirming quality, configuration, material identity, and authorized approvals remain intact. Options can include resequencing, controlled overtime, cross-training, alternate qualified resource, supplier escalation, or customer/program decision. Do not create hidden WIP or bypass controls to improve a date.

## Exit

The near-term schedule is credible, risks are explicitly owned, and a miss is explained by a testable cause category with a response path.
