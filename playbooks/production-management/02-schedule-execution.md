# Schedule execution

## Question answered

What should be built now, what can actually be built, and what decision closes the gap without compromising the product?

## Reconcile daily

Compare demand/commitment, released work, configuration-effective plan, actual WIP status, material/tool/people/equipment readiness, expected yield, constraints, inspection/test holds, and downstream capacity. Use reason codes that identify the mechanism: shortage, qualification, hold, equipment, yield, or capacity.

## Recovery rule

Choose a recovery action only after confirming quality, configuration, material identity, and authorized approvals remain intact. Options can include resequencing, controlled overtime, cross-training, alternate qualified resource, supplier escalation, or customer/program decision. Do not create hidden WIP or bypass controls to improve a date.

## Exit

The near-term schedule states its readiness risks, owners, cause categories, and response path.
