# Visual performance management

## Question answered

Can the people who control the work see current condition, planned condition, abnormality, and the next action quickly enough to act?

## Design principles

Put the information at the decision point; show plan versus actual, trend, definition, owner, and next action; distinguish real-time operational control from retrospective reporting; use stable visual signals; and link a red condition to a reaction/escalation path.

## Anti-patterns

Lagging dashboards with no decision, manual numbers nobody trusts, targets without baselines, local optimization that hides WIP/rework, and visual boards that create parallel uncontrolled records.

## Exit

Each displayed measure triggers a known operating response and is reconciled to an authoritative source; stale or unused measures are removed.
