# Material and execution readiness

## Question answered

Is work ready to start and complete with the right material, status, information, tooling, people, and downstream path?

## Readiness check

For each near-term job verify effective configuration, released route/instructions, material identity/quantity/status, kit completeness, tooling/program/equipment, qualified labor, inspection/test availability, required approvals, and physical/digital location. Separate hard blockers from uncertainty that needs verification.

## Shortage discipline

Classify shortages by effect on the constraint/customer, availability date confidence, substitute/alternate qualification, and ownership. Keep a shortage record linked to affected orders/configurations rather than a freeform email queue.

## Exit

The team records what can run, what cannot, why, recovery owner, and escalation time from source evidence.
