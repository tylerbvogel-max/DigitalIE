# Constraint control

## Question answered

What limits system throughput now, and how can the organization protect and improve it without creating excess inventory or quality risk elsewhere?

## Observe before optimizing

Identify demand, actual throughput, queue age, starvation/blockage, downtime, changeovers, yield/rework, shared resources, release policy, and product-mix effects. The apparent busiest machine is not necessarily the system constraint.

## Operating rules

Protect the constraint from avoidable waiting; ensure it receives ready, conforming work; subordinate release and support work to its realistic rate; and measure loss mechanisms at the constraint. Elevate capacity only after simpler losses are understood.

## Exit

One verified constraint hypothesis, a loss mechanism, a controlled improvement, and an expected system-level outcome—not a local utilization win.
