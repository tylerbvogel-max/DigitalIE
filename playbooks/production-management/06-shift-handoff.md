# Shift handoff

## Question answered

Can the incoming team safely and correctly continue the work without rediscovering status, risk, and decisions?

## Required handoff content

Plan/actual and current constraint; work in process identity/status/location; abnormal conditions and containment; quality/inspection/test holds; shortages and expected arrivals; equipment/tool condition; configuration/change cut-ins; safety concerns; commitments due next shift; and decisions/escalations awaiting action.

## Design

Use a shared, time-stamped record plus a brief face-to-face or equivalent review for critical work. Capture source links and ownership; do not force an incoming supervisor to decode abbreviations or unstructured chat history.

## Exit

Incoming ownership is explicit, critical information is acknowledged, and unresolved items retain their identity and escalation clock.
