# Labor, skills, and coverage

## Question answered

Does the production plan have the qualified, available, sustainably loaded capability required to execute it?

## Model capability

Map required work content and takt/cycle demand against actual attendance, qualifications, skill levels, cross-training, supervision, ergonomic/safety constraints, planned absence, overtime, and learning curve. A headcount count does not reveal qualified capacity.

## Use a skill matrix

For each critical operation, show authorized/qualified people, proficiency, recency, trainer, expiration/requalification, and single-point-of-failure exposure. Treat cross-training as resilience work tied to constraint and risk—not generic development.

## Exit

The staffing plan states its qualification assumptions, coverage gaps, mitigation, and risk owner; overtime and reassignment are evaluated for their quality/safety consequence.
