# Aerospace change control

## Question answered

What must change, who must approve it, what existing product is affected, and how will the organization avoid mixing old and new configurations?

## Impact map

For any design, process, tooling, software/program, supplier, inspection, documentation, or planning change, assess: applicable approvals; configuration/effectivity; WIP and inventory; material/lot; routing/work instructions; training/qualification; tooling/test equipment; FMEA/control plan; supplier flow-down; data/system master records; customer/regulator communication; and verification evidence.

## Controlled cut-in

Define old/new boundary by serial, lot, date, order, or configuration—not by vague calendar intent. Identify segregate/rework/scrap/return choices for WIP and inventory through authorized disposition. Verify that all point-of-use information changes together.

## Exit

The change record contains authority, effective scope, affected-product disposition, implementation evidence, verification result, and rollback/containment plan where feasible.
