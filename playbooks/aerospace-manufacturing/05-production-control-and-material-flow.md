# Production control and material flow

## Question answered

Can work flow predictably to the needed configuration and date while preserving identity, condition, and quality status?

## Model the flow

Map demand, release, material availability, kit completeness, routing, queue/WIP, constraints, inspection/test holds, rework loops, move/transaction rules, and shipment or next-customer handoff. Distinguish a material shortage from an information, approval, capacity, or configuration constraint.

## Daily operating questions

- What will constrain the next 24–72 hours, and what evidence supports that prediction?
- Which shortages threaten the true constraint or customer commitment?
- Is WIP waiting on work, data, engineering, inspection, material, or a disposition?
- Which expedites bypass normal controls, and how is their risk contained?
- Does the schedule reflect actual configuration, yield, and capacity assumptions?

## Guardrail

Never resolve a schedule miss by losing traveler identity, mixing status, bypassing required inspection, or consuming material with uncertain pedigree.

## Exit

The operating team has one shared constraint/shortage view, named owners, an escalation path, and metrics for schedule adherence, WIP age, shortages, rework, and quality holds.
