# Manufacturing readiness and process planning

## Question answered

Is the operation genuinely ready to build a conforming article repeatedly—not merely ready to start work?

## Readiness review

Check approved/effective design data; process plan and point-of-use instructions; qualified people; tooling/fixtures/programs; material availability and traceability; measurement/inspection capability; special-process path; test equipment; ERP/MES/master-data setup; supplier readiness; packaging/handling; and abnormality/reaction paths.

## Evidence standard

For each readiness item, record requirement, accountable owner, objective evidence, affected configuration, unresolved risk, and release decision. “Someone said it is ready” is not evidence.

## Learn by controlled build

Use a first build, pilot, transfer, or production-readiness review to compare planned versus actual time, touch points, defects, workarounds, missing information, and training demand. Feed discoveries back into planning, FMEA, standard work, and control plans before scale.

## Exit

The release decision has explicit scope and residual risks; blockers have owners and dates; actual-build learning has a closed feedback route.
