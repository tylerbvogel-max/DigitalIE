# Supplier quality and external process control

## Question answered

Can the organization rely on externally supplied material, parts, and processes with evidence proportional to their risk?

## Supplier control loop

Segment suppliers/processes by product criticality, process risk, historical performance, sole-source exposure, and detection ability. Define approved-source requirements, flow-down clauses, first-article/source-inspection needs, certificate requirements, receiving verification, performance reviews, and escalation routes.

## When a discrepancy appears

Contain affected receipts/WIP; identify lot/serial and downstream scope; preserve records; determine whether the issue is supplier-caused, specification/flow-down ambiguity, handling damage, or receiving-detection failure; then use the appropriate supplier corrective-action process.

## Measures

On-time delivery, incoming defect/escape rate, responsiveness, repeat findings, certificate completeness, corrective-action aging, source-inspection outcomes, and risk-weighted supplier health.

## Exit

Supplier status, affected-product scope, containment, and the required corrective/evidence path are explicit. Supplier performance is a joint quality-and-flow problem, not procurement’s private scorecard.
