# Critical-process governance

## Question answered

Is a process whose output cannot be fully verified later being performed under the required qualified, controlled, and evidenced conditions?

## Scope carefully

“Special” and “critical” process labels are controlled by contract, program, customer, and organizational requirements. Examples can include heat treatment, welding, coatings, composites, nondestructive testing, chemical processing, materials testing, and measurement/inspection. This playbook governs the control questions; it never supplies technical process parameters.

## Control chain

Approved procedure and revision → qualified facility/person/equipment → verified inputs → controlled parameters/environment → required monitoring → certificate/record → acceptance/release.

## Questions

- Is the required procedure, qualification, accreditation, and scope current for this process/product?
- Can every certificate be joined to the exact product, lot, quantity, and required specification revision?
- Are equipment calibration, personnel qualification, and environmental controls in date at the time of execution?
- What happens when a certificate, parameter, or traceability link is incomplete?

## Exit

Missing evidence or uncertain scope holds the product for authorized review. Do not recreate a certificate or infer compliance from later outcomes.
