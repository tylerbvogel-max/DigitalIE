# Operations data and AI

## Question answered

Can operational data or AI reduce time-to-understanding without degrading traceability, controlled decision rights, security, or conformance?

## Start with a decision

Specify the user, decision, action window, required source evidence, error cost, and human authority. A dashboard that cannot change a decision—or whose definitions no one trusts—is reporting exhaust, not an operational tool.

## Data contract

For every metric or recommendation define source system, grain/key, operational definition, refresh/latency, quality checks, owner, authorized audience, retention/export constraints, and known blind spots. Preserve links to source records rather than replacing them with model summaries.

## AI boundaries

AI may summarize approved records, surface anomalies, draft questions, assemble evidence, and propose analyses. It may not authorize release, disposition nonconforming product, determine technical compliance, alter controlled data, or substitute for an authorized engineering/quality/airworthiness finding.

## Exit

The solution has a measurable decision benefit, source lineage, named owner, human review boundary, access/security review, and a method to detect harmful or stale recommendations.
