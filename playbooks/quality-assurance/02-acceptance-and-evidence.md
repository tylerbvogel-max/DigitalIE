# Acceptance and objective evidence

## Question answered

What requirement is being accepted, by what valid method, with what evidence, and by whom?

## Acceptance chain

Requirement and revision → defined characteristic → capable measurement/test/inspection → recorded result → acceptance criterion → authorized status/release.

## Evidence quality

Evidence must be attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original or controlled copy, and traceable to the item/process/configuration. Define sampling, uncertainty, retest rules, data review, and electronic-signature/authorization rules before the result is needed.

## Failure modes

Checking without an operational definition; accepting based on a later reconstruction; treating calibrated as automatically fit-for-purpose; losing link between result and unit/lot; or allowing a dashboard aggregate to replace the originating record.

## Exit

The acceptance decision can be reproduced from governing requirement through source evidence by an authorized reviewer.
