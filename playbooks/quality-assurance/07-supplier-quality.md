# Supplier quality

## Question answered

How are externally provided products and processes planned, verified, monitored, and improved according to their risk?

## Quality plan

Translate requirements into approved-source criteria, technical/quality flow-down, certificate and traceability requirements, first-article or source-inspection plan, receiving verification, change-notification expectations, performance review, and corrective-action escalation.

## Avoid false ownership boundaries

Procurement owns commercial relationship mechanics; quality owns conformance system adequacy; engineering owns technical requirement clarity; operations owns material planning and use. A supplier escape often crosses all four.

## Exit

Risk-based supplier controls, current evidence, performance signals, and escalation ownership are visible before a receipt becomes a production crisis.
