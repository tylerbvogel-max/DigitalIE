# Quality system and quality planning

## Question answered

Does the organization have a coherent, owned system that translates customer/regulatory/design requirements into controlled work, evidence, learning, and management action?

## Map the system

Identify interested parties and requirements; process map and owners; policy/objectives; risk/opportunity planning; competence; controlled information; operational controls; supplier controls; inspection/measurement; nonconformance/CAPA; audits; management review; and continual improvement.

For a specific product/process, build a quality plan that joins CTQs, requirements, process steps, acceptance method, records, reaction path, owner, and change trigger.

## Questions

- Can a floor operator find the current requirement at the decision point?
- Is each handoff clear on its acceptance criteria and evidence?
- Are exceptions visible, authorized, time-bound, and reviewed?
- Does management review trends, systemic risk, action effectiveness, and open-item counts?

## Exit

The process has an accountable owner, a requirement-to-control path, and feedback from actual performance into risk, plans, and management decisions.
