# Nonconformance control

## Question answered

How is a condition that fails a requirement identified, contained, evaluated, dispositioned, and prevented from being used or released unintentionally?

## Immediate loop

Identify and preserve product/process evidence; label/segregate or digitally block it; define scope; notify required owners; record objective condition and requirement; evaluate disposition through authorized roles; implement disposition; verify it; and decide whether CAPA/escalation is warranted.

## Diagnostic questions

- What precisely is nonconforming, compared with which requirement?
- Is containment physical, digital, and communicative?
- Which serials/lots/orders/records share the exposure?
- Is rework/repair/use-as-is/scrap/return decision authorized and evidenced?
- Did the condition escape a prior control, and does that create a broader risk?

## Exit

No product remains ambiguous in status. The disposition is authorized, implemented, verified, and connected to systemic learning when the risk warrants it.
