# Quality metrics and management review

## Question answered

What does leadership need to know about quality-system health, process risk, and action effectiveness—and what decision follows?

## Metric design

For every measure define decision, owner, operational definition, numerator/denominator, population, source, latency, exclusions, target/baseline, and predictable response. Combine outcome measures (escapes, defects, COPQ) with process-health measures (audit effectiveness, CAPA aging/effectiveness, calibration status, supplier risk, first-pass yield).

## Management review

Review trends and risk, not decorative red/yellow/green. Ask what changed; where controls are weak; which recurring conditions warrant systemic action; whether resources/competence are adequate; and whether previous actions worked.

## Exit

Every management decision has an owner, due date, expected outcome, and review evidence. Metrics that do not support a decision are retired or redesigned.
