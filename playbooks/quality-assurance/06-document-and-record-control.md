# Document and record control

## Question answered

Can people use the current approved information, and can the organization later prove what occurred?

## Distinction

A **document** tells work what should happen. A **record** evidences what did happen. Both require identity, revision/status, owner, access, retention/protection, and change control; records additionally require integrity and retrievability over their retention life.

## Control questions

- Who approves, releases, changes, and withdraws each controlled document?
- How is the current version made available at point of use and obsolete versions prevented from use?
- What is the authoritative system of record versus a convenience copy?
- Which key fields/links make a record retrievable by product, process, date, and requirement?
- How are corrections attributable without erasing the original history?

## Exit

The team can identify the governing version and retrieve complete, protected evidence without reconstructing it from emails or tribal memory.
