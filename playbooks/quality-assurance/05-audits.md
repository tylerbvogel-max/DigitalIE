# Audits: test the operating system

## Question answered

Is the defined system both followed and effective at achieving its intended outcome?

## Audit design

Set scope, criteria, process risk, evidence sample, independence, and reporting route. Follow a transaction end-to-end: requirement, planning, execution, evidence, exception, decision, and feedback. Audit trails reveal system behavior better than clause-by-clause interviewing alone.

## Interview and evidence discipline

Ask people to show the work and records. Record objective evidence, requirement/criterion, condition, and risk—not personality judgments or proposed root causes. Distinguish an isolated observation from a systemic finding.

## Exit

Findings have clear evidence, accountable owners, appropriate correction/CAPA routing, due dates, and verified closure. Audit trends inform management review and risk planning.
