# Technical risk management

## Question answered

What future performance shortfall might occur, why, with what consequence, and what evidence-triggered action protects the commitment?

## Risk statement

`Given condition, there is a possibility that event occurs, leading to consequence against an explicit objective.`

Record evidence, likelihood basis, consequence dimensions, exposure window, owner, handling strategy, mitigation, contingency, trigger threshold, residual risk, and decision authority.

## Control loop

Identify → analyze → select handling → assign action/trigger → monitor leading evidence → execute mitigation or contingency → decide residual risk → close or transfer.

Risks are future uncertainties. Realized conditions route to exception, recovery, or corrective-action processes. A problem list with red/yellow/green colors is not risk management.

## Exit

The risk has an objective at risk, causal basis, owner, actionable threshold, funded handling path, review cadence, and recorded residual-risk decision.
