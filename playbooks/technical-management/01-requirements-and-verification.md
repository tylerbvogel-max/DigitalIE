# Requirements and verification management

## Question answered

Can each obligation be traced from an authoritative source to an implementable requirement, verification method, acceptance result, and approved residual exception?

## Control chain

Stakeholder/contract need → allocated requirement → implementation definition → verification method → objective result → authorized acceptance or exception.

## Diagnostic questions

- What is the authoritative source, exact revision, applicability, and effectivity?
- Is the statement singular, unambiguous, feasible, necessary, and verifiable?
- Who owns the requirement and who accepts its verification result?
- Are parent, child, interface, derived, and verification relationships explicit?
- Does a changed requirement identify every affected design, process, tool, supplier, inspection, test, record, and delivered configuration?
- Are verification and validation being confused: did we build to the requirement, and does the result satisfy the intended use?

## Exit

Applicable requirements have stable identity, source, ownership, allocation, method, status, evidence, and approved exception path. An unverified or conflicting obligation is visible risk, not presumed compliance.
