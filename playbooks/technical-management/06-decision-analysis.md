# Formal decision analysis

## Question answered

For a consequential choice, can leadership see the objective, alternatives, evidence, uncertainty, tradeoffs, authority, and resulting commitment?

## Decision frame

State decision owner, deadline, objective, constraints, non-negotiable thresholds, alternatives including status quo, criteria and weights, assumptions, evidence quality, uncertainty, reversibility, second-order effects, and dissent.

Assess safety, technical performance, conformance, configuration, schedule, cost, workforce, supplier, data/security, customer, and residual-risk impacts at the rigor appropriate to the decision.

## Guardrails

- Do not backfit criteria to a preferred answer.
- Distinguish a missing alternative from a rejected alternative.
- Show sensitivity to uncertain assumptions and weights.
- Preserve minority concerns that could change the decision if later evidence validates them.
- Record who decided, the basis, conditions, actions, and revisit triggers.

## Exit

The authorized decision, rationale, assumptions, dissent, implementation owner, verification, and revisit condition are recorded.
