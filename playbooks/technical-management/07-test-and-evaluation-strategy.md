# Test and evaluation strategy

## Question answered

What combination of analysis, inspection, demonstration, and test will produce sufficient evidence that requirements are satisfied across the intended operational and manufacturing envelope?

## Build the evidence argument

For each requirement record source/revision, allocation, verification method, level, article/configuration, environment, instrumentation and measurement capability, procedure, sample rationale, acceptance criteria, independence, prerequisites, data/record owner, anomaly path, and approving authority. Trace verification evidence upward and unresolved failures back to affected requirements and configurations.

Plan developmental learning separately from formal acceptance evidence. Define how models, similarity, heritage, qualification articles, coupons, first articles, production acceptance tests, and inspections may support a claim and where they cannot. Identify destructive-test implications, limited assets, sequence dependencies, environmental conditioning, retest rules, and multiple-comparison or data-reuse risks.

## Readiness and execution

Before execution verify approved configuration, procedure, calibrated equipment, trained personnel, facilities, safety controls, software/data acquisition, test limits, abort criteria, anomaly authority, and record retention. Preserve raw data and transformations. Never discard an anomalous run solely because a later run passed.

## Output

Requirement-to-evidence matrix, test architecture, assumptions, sample and uncertainty rationale, readiness evidence, raw/result lineage, anomalies/deviations, coverage gaps, conclusion by requirement, residual risk, and authorized acceptance.

Public orientation: [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/nasa_systems_engineering_handbook_0.pdf) and [INCOSE Systems Engineering Handbook overview](https://www.incose.org/resources-publications/technical-publications/se-handbook/). Applicable program, customer, regulatory, and safety requirements control.
