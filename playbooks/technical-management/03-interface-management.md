# Interface management

## Question answered

Are the technical and organizational boundaries required for product realization defined, owned, controlled, verified, and monitored?

## Interface record

Identify provider, receiver, exchanged item/information/service, requirement, format/unit/tolerance, timing, configuration, acceptance method, owner on each side, change authority, and failure reaction.

Interfaces include product geometry, hardware/software, tooling, material, facility, data, supplier/customer, organizational handoff, and human-machine boundaries.

## Diagnostic questions

- Does each side use the same identifier, unit, revision, timing, and acceptance meaning?
- Is the interface controlled in both source systems or reconciled to one authority?
- What detects missing, late, malformed, stale, or conflicting payloads?
- Who assesses downstream effect when either side changes?
- Has the integrated interface been verified under realistic conditions, including failure and recovery?

## Exit

Both sides acknowledge the controlled interface, verification evidence, ownership, monitoring, and change route. Unowned boundary failures remain open risks.
