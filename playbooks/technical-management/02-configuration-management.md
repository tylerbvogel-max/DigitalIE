# Configuration management

## Question answered

What is the approved product and process definition at this point in time, and can the organization prove that every affected artifact and article is consistent with it?

## Five functions

1. Plan configuration governance and authority.
2. Identify configuration items, baselines, versions, and effectivity.
3. Control proposed changes and their impact.
4. Account for status: proposed, approved, implemented, verified, and superseded.
5. Verify configuration through functional/physical review and audit appropriate to the lifecycle.

## Four synchronized views

`as-designed → as-planned → as-built/as-inspected → as-operated/as-maintained`

## Failure signals

Latest-revision assumptions, undocumented redlines, ambiguous effectivity, partial point-of-use release, WIP caught between baselines, software/tooling version mismatch, and records that cannot reconstruct the delivered configuration.

## Exit

The applicable baseline, approved change authority, effectivity, implementation status, affected product, and audit evidence are explicit. Configuration uncertainty stops release or change execution at the appropriate authority gate.
