# Triage and problem framing

## First question: contain or learn?

Contain immediately when there is credible safety exposure, escaped quality risk, customer commitment risk, regulatory risk, or ongoing material loss. Record the containment as temporary; it is not a corrective action.

## Good problem statement

State: *what* is abnormal, *where*, *when*, *against what expected condition*, and *the quantified impact*. Avoid proposed causes and blame.

> From 11–14 August, final-assembly station 40 on shift B recorded 4.8% torque-incomplete results against a 0.4% baseline, delaying 23 units for rework.

## Minimum evidence packet

- Operational definition of the defect/delay.
- Baseline and current metric with denominator and time window.
- Affected process, product/configuration, shift, and boundary.
- Containment status and owner.
- Known changes around the onset date.
- What is directly observed versus reported secondhand.

## Exit condition

The team agrees on the effect to investigate, the process boundary, the immediate risk disposition, and the next evidence-collection step.
