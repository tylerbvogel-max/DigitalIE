# Control plan: sustain the new normal

## Purpose

Convert a verified improvement or FMEA control into ordinary work that detects abnormality early and directs a useful response. A control plan without an executable reaction plan is filing, not control.

## Minimum field structure

| Process / characteristic | Requirement / limit | Method and source | Frequency / sample | Owner | Normal action | Abnormal reaction | Escalation / record |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For every critical characteristic, distinguish the engineering/customer requirement from an SPC control limit. Include the reaction to suspect material produced since the last known-good check.

## Reaction plan

Specify: stop/contain trigger; identification and segregation method; who may restart; first check after adjustment; required record; and escalation if the abnormality repeats. Make it feasible at the point of work.

## Verification

Before closing an improvement, verify that the control is trained, available, used under real conditions, auditable, and effective over a stated period or exposure count. An action is not effective merely because it was installed.

## Exit

The process has an owned detection/reaction loop, a review cadence, and a trigger to revisit the FMEA or standard work when conditions change.
