# Gemba: understand work where it happens

## Objective

Compare standard work, actual work, and process outcome without treating the operator as the defect.

## Before the walk

Define the question, safety/access rules, observation window, affected product/process, and who needs to be present. Read the current work instruction, but do not assume it describes reality.

## Observe and ask

Record time, station, configuration, sequence, handoffs, waiting, interruptions, search/reach/walk, rework, equipment state, material presentation, information used, and environmental conditions. Ask:

- “Can you show me the normal sequence?”
- “Where does this become difficult or ambiguous?”
- “What do you do when the normal path does not work?”
- “What would make this easier to do correctly the first time?”

Capture quotes as attributed observations, not conclusions. Avoid “why did you” questions that imply fault.

## Look for

- Workarounds that compensate for missing information, tooling, material, or capability.
- Difference between work instruction, training, and actual sequence.
- Batch/release queues and repeated handoffs.
- Sources of variation by product, shift, operator role, equipment, or condition.

## Exit

Produce an observation log, a list of unknowns, and candidate hypotheses/tests. Do not leave with a declared root cause.
