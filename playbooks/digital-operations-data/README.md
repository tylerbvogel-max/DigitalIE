# Digital Operations and Data field playbooks

Digital operations makes the operating system observable and easier to improve. It starts from an operational decision and source evidence—not a preferred dashboard, platform, or AI capability.

| Connect systems safely | [Integration and lineage](03-integration-and-lineage.md) |
| Build usable operational visibility | [Dashboards and alerts](04-dashboards-and-alerts.md) |
| Apply AI to an operations decision | [AI decision support](05-ai-decision-support.md) |
| Govern access, quality, and lifecycle | [Data governance and operations](06-data-governance-and-operations.md) |
