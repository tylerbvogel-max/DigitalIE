## Precedence and trust

- Which sources may instruct behavior?
- Which sources are untrusted content only?
- How are conflicts surfaced?
- What must remain verbatim versus summarized?
- What triggers retrieval, compaction, invalidation, or re-verification?

## Evaluation

Test missing critical context, excessive low-signal context, stale sources, conflicting authority, malicious embedded instructions, and long-horizon handoff loss.
