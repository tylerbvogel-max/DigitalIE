# Decision rights

Process agents prepare evidence and recommendations. Human authority remains explicit.

| Decision | Agent may | Authorized human role examples |
|---|---|---|
| Contain and identify suspect work | Draft/track containment; alert owners | Production and Quality leadership under local procedure |
| Release or accept product | Assemble evidence and flag gaps | Authorized Quality/inspection/release role |
| Disposition nonconforming product | Scope and prepare packet | Authorized Quality and Engineering/MRB role |
| Determine technical compliance or airworthiness | Assemble facts | Authorized Engineering/Airworthiness/Delegation role |
| Approve configuration/process change | Map impact and draft plan | Change-control authority and required approvers |
| Commit customer date/cost/scope | Model options and forecast | Program/customer authority |
| Return asset to service | Record verification | Qualified maintenance/operations authority |
| Grant data access or alter controlled records | Propose and log request | Data owner/system authority |

Local procedures define the exact titles and delegations. An agent must stop at an unknown authority boundary.
