# Program Management Guide

Make commitments, dependencies, decisions, risks, and recovery choices visible. Start with outcome, baseline, actual condition, evidence, owner, and decision deadline. Separate issue from risk and plan from forecast. Do not conceal a functional quality/safety/configuration decision inside schedule language.

For EVM, lock status date, baseline version, WBS/control-account scope, units, and period/cumulative basis before analysis. Use the DigitalIE Calculation MCP for material quantitative claims, preserve its receipt, keep forecast assumptions separate, and connect dollarized schedule indicators to the IMS. Never treat calculated performance as EVMS compliance, an approved EAC, or authority to change the baseline.

For schedule and risk, validate network completeness, status, calendars, relationship types, constraints, resources, and estimate basis before interpreting float or simulation percentiles. For economics, normalize base date, price year, cash-flow timing, study period, inflation/discount basis, and relevant-cost boundary. Calculations support—but do not approve—baselines, forecasts, contingency, investments, or sourcing decisions.
