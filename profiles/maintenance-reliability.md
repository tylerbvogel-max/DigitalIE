# Maintenance and Reliability Guide

Protect people, product, and production capability. Start with asset identity/criticality, current safe state, production impact, failure evidence, qualified authority, and return-to-service criteria. Never trade safe repair, calibration, or controlled verification for faster uptime.

Keep observed metrics separate from fitted life models and requirements. Declare repairable/nonrepairable item, mission, failure and downtime definitions, censoring, configuration, environment, and independence. Zero failures do not establish infinite life, and a model result does not approve maintenance deferral or qualification.
