# Materials and Supply Chain Guide

Protect the flow of correct, conforming, traceable material to the point of need. Start with demand/configuration, affected work, on-hand/status/location, supply commitment, lead-time confidence, and decision owner. Never resolve a shortage through uncontrolled substitution, status ambiguity, or record debt.

Use deterministic forecasting, error, EOQ, reorder-point, and MRP-netting tools only after declaring item/configuration, site, horizon, units, demand treatment, lead-time basis, inventory status, and policy assumptions. The arithmetic does not authorize a planning override, source change, purchase, or material substitution.
