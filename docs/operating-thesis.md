# Operating thesis

DigitalIE exists to improve the quality and speed of manufacturing decisions without becoming a shadow quality system, engineering authority, or system of record.

## Decisions it supports

- Is work ready to release to production?
- Does the available evidence support acceptance or require an exception path?
- What product, configuration, material, or commitment is exposed by an abnormal condition?
- What change is required, who may authorize it, and where must it take effect?
- Which constraint governs recovery, and which tradeoffs require escalation?
- What evidence distinguishes a plausible cause from a verified cause?
- Which data product shortens a defined decision without obscuring provenance?

## Value hypothesis

DigitalIE should reduce decision latency, containment latency, exception aging, repeated failure, readiness misses, and manual evidence assembly. It should improve first-pass acceptance, schedule credibility, configuration integrity, action closure, and corrective-action effectiveness.

A claimed benefit requires a named baseline, scope, mechanism, owner, comparison period, and observed result. Document volume, prompts run, dashboards published, and agent output count are not benefits.

## Governing boundary

Approved employer procedures, contracts, customer requirements, regulatory obligations, delegated authority, and systems of record govern real work. DigitalIE supplies questions, methods, evidence structures, and decision preparation. It does not authorize product release, disposition, engineering changes, airworthiness findings, controlled-record changes, customer commitments, or data access.

## Proof standard

Every intervention should state:

1. the decision or operating outcome being changed;
2. the baseline and exposure;
3. the proposed causal mechanism;
4. expected benefit and credible downside;
5. the authority required;
6. the measurement and review window; and
7. the result, including null or adverse outcomes.

## Design test

Retain an artifact only when it improves a real decision, preserves evidence, clarifies authority, enables a reusable action, or tests whether an intervention worked.
