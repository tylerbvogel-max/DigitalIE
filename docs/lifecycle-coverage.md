# Lifecycle and risk coverage

This matrix prevents corpus growth from following department boundaries or author familiarity. `Strong` means a routed playbook/process and usable field questions exist. `Partial` means the topic is present but lacks a full process contract or artifact set. `Gap` is intentional work to prioritize only when use demonstrates need.

## Current crosscutting gaps

- Bid-to-manufacturing requirement flow and industrial-cost assumptions
- System-level integration of human factors, ergonomics, and workforce qualification across process handoffs
- Integration of facility decisions with environmental, EHS, and emergency-operating authorities
- Product support, repair, overhaul, service data, and end-of-life configuration
- Counterfeit-part avoidance and deeper supply-chain assurance
- Cyber-physical manufacturing security and operational-technology change control
- Model-based definition and model-based enterprise assurance

Do not add a new family merely to turn every cell green. Add one when a recurring decision lacks a safe route, usable evidence contract, or competent critic.
