# Capability source crosswalk

## Purpose

This ledger explains why a capability belongs in DigitalIE. It uses public curricula and certification outlines as coverage probes, not as local requirements, rankings, or substitutes for controlled standards. A source can reveal a missing competency; it cannot grant authority or prove that a DigitalIE method is correct.

## Admission rule

A proposed capability enters the corpus only when it has:

1. a manufacturing decision or failure mode it improves;
2. a public primary orientation source or an explicitly identified controlled source;
3. a separation between deterministic calculation and professional judgment;
4. declared inputs, assumptions, failure behavior, outputs, and authority boundary;
5. a playbook or skill for interpretation;
6. golden, boundary, and rejection tests for any calculation;
7. a path to an improvement case, evidence IDs, and human review.

Do not reproduce paid bodies of knowledge or controlled aerospace requirements. Record identifiers and applicability, then defer to the licensed current source and the organization's approved procedures.

| Design of experiments | effects, interactions, ANOVA | experimental design, randomization, causal limits | add quality/IE pack |

| Reliability | life distributions, availability, system reliability | failure definition, censoring, model fitness, maintenance strategy | deepen reliability family |
| Engineering economics | cash-flow equivalence, NPV, IRR, break-even, life-cycle cost | alternatives, estimate basis, uncertainty, approval | add cross-functional pack |
| Operations research | typed optimization and simulation | formulation, constraints, objective, validation, sensitivity | add IE modeling skill |
| Human factors/work design | work-sample and approved ergonomic equations | observation, task boundary, safety/medical authority | add IE skill; constrain calculations |

| Technology insertion | economic/capacity scenarios | maturity, integration, qualification, cutover, rollback | add aerospace skill |

## Known exclusions

DigitalIE does not certify people or organizations, interpret proprietary standards without licensed access, provide legal conclusions, approve product, select undocumented process parameters, or replace authorized engineering, quality, safety, airworthiness, or program decisions.

Review this ledger when sources revise their curricula or when operational evidence shows a missing decision capability. Addition count is not a success metric; verified decision usefulness is.
