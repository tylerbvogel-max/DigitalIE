# Capacity and line balancing

**Use when:** required demand, resource capacity, work balance, or governing constraint is disputed.

**Do:** define mix, net time, demand, observed cycle/work content, availability, yield, changeover, skills, and variability; calculate takt, theoretical minimum operators, expected good capacity, and WIP/lead-time relationship; observe starvation, blockage, and queues before naming the constraint.

**Stop when:** nameplate rates replace demonstrated performance, averages conceal product mix, or a balance transfers safety, quality, ergonomic, qualification, or handoff burden.

**Output:** formulas/inputs, demand-capacity scenarios, constraint evidence, assumptions/sensitivity, and controlled improvement. See [the playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/13-capacity-and-line-balancing.md).
