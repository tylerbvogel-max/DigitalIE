# Descriptive statistics

**Use when:** a manufacturing dataset needs frequency, center, spread, or graphical description before inference.

**Do:** preserve population, unit, time order, strata, exclusions, and units; compute \(f_j\), \(p_j=f_j/n\), mean \(\bar x=\sum x_i/n\), median, mode where useful, range, sample variance \(s^2=\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2/(n-1)\), standard deviation, and IQR; choose a graph from the operational question and show raw variation.

**Stop when:** measurement integrity, denominator, sampling unit, missingness, or category definitions are unresolved.

**Output:** data reference, formulas/methods, full-precision result, graph construction, anomalies/strata, and decision relevance. See [the calculation playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/15-descriptive-statistics-and-graphs.md).
