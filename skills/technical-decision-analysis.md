# Technical decision analysis

**Use when:** a consequential choice has competing alternatives, uncertain evidence, or cross-domain impacts.

**Do:** name decision owner/deadline; define objective, constraints, alternatives, criteria, assumptions, uncertainty, reversibility, dissent, and sensitivity; map implementation and revisit triggers.

**Stop when:** criteria conceal a controlled threshold, authority is unclear, or evidence cannot support the requested choice.

**Output:** decision frame, evaluated alternatives, sensitivity, dissent, recommendation, and approval packet.
