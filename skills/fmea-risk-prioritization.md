# FMEA risk prioritization

**Use when:** anticipating failure modes before launch, transfer, or change.

**Do:** separate function, failure mode, effect, mechanism, prevention, and detection; use locally defined severity/occurrence/detection criteria; if required calculate \(RPN=S\times O\times D\) while preserving components and high-severity escalation; connect actions to control-plan change and verification.

**Stop when:** RPN alone determines acceptance, severity is diluted by low occurrence, detection is mistaken for prevention, or scoring replaces evidence.

**Output:** risk chain, component ratings/rationale, action priority, owner/date, control change, and verification. See [the playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/08-fmea.md).
