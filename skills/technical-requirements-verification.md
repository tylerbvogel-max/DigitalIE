# Requirements and verification

**Use when:** an obligation, acceptance basis, allocation, or verification result is unclear.

**Do:** identify authoritative source/revision/effectivity; trace parent-child-interface relationships; distinguish verification from validation; map requirement to method, evidence, status, and acceptance authority.

**Stop when:** applicability, conflict resolution, waiver/deviation, or acceptance requires an authorized technical or customer decision.

**Output:** requirement-evidence map, conflict/gap list, verification status, and decision request.
