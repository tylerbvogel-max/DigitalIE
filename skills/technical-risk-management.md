# Technical risk management

**Use when:** uncertainty could create a future performance shortfall against safety, technical, cost, schedule, or mission objectives.

**Do:** write condition-event-consequence; record evidence and uncertainty; define owner, mitigation, contingency, trigger, exposure window, monitoring, and residual-risk decision.

**Stop when:** the event has occurred, authority must accept risk, or handling conflicts with controlled requirements.

**Output:** risk statement, handling plan, trigger evidence, status, and decision request.
