# Aerospace producibility and DFMA

**Use when:** a design, change, transfer, rate increase, recurring defect, or supply constraint may be driven by how the product is manufactured, assembled, inspected, tested, or repaired.

**Do:** trace requirements and critical characteristics into process capability, tooling, access, tolerance, joining, inspection, test, handling, repair, supplier, and qualification needs; compare alternatives across technical performance, conformity, rate, cost, schedule, maintainability, and risk; connect accepted changes to configuration control.

**Stop when:** design authority, certification basis, key characteristics, material/process specifications, or product acceptance are implicated.

**Output:** evidence-linked producibility review, trade results and receipts, affected configuration, verification/qualification impact, recommendation, dissent, and approval packet. See [the playbook](../playbooks/aerospace-manufacturing/10-producibility-and-dfma.md).
