# Aerospace material and process qualification

**Use when:** selecting, qualifying, transferring, changing, or recovering a material, special/critical process, facility, equipment set, parameter window, or inspection method.

**Do:** establish approved requirements and authority; define the application envelope, process physics, failure mechanisms, controlled variables, representativeness, sample rationale, acceptance evidence, production controls, equivalence, and requalification triggers; preserve configuration and lot/serial traceability.

**Stop when:** applicable controlled requirements are unavailable, the proposed evidence does not represent production, an excursion affects product, or qualification/acceptance requires customer or engineering approval.

**Output:** evidence-linked qualification plan or gap assessment, declared calculations, applicability/limitations, control plan, requalification triggers, record package, and approval route. See [the playbook](../playbooks/aerospace-manufacturing/11-material-and-process-qualification.md).
