# Correlation and regression

**Use when:** two continuous variables may have a linear association or a response is being modeled from one predictor.

**Do:** plot paired data and strata; calculate \(S_{xx},S_{yy},S_{xy}\), \(r=S_{xy}/\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}\), \(b_1=S_{xy}/S_{xx}\), \(b_0=\bar y-b_1\bar x\), fitted values, residuals, SSE/SSR/SST, \(R^2\), slope SE/test, and residual diagnostics; state valid range and mechanism limits.

**Stop when:** pairing, time order, omitted strata, nonlinearity, dependence, changing variance, influential points, or extrapolation make the stated model misleading.

**Output:** scatterplot, equation with units/range, correlation, coefficient intervals/tests, determination, residual evidence, sensitivity, and decision relevance. See [the regression playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/19-correlation-and-regression.md).
