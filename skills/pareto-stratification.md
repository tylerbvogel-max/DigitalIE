# Pareto and stratification

**Use when:** loss may be concentrated by category, product, station, configuration, supplier, shift, or time.

**Do:** define event and exposure; compute \(Share_j=x_j/\sum x_k\), ordered cumulative share, and category-specific rate \(d_j/N_j\); compare count and impact separately; expose “other,” category changes, and volume effects.

**Stop when:** categories overlap, denominators differ without rate adjustment, or a category label is being treated as a cause.

**Output:** frequency/impact/rate tables, Pareto construction, strata, vital investigation targets, and limits. See [the playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/04-pareto-and-stratification.md).
