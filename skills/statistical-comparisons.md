# Statistical comparisons

**Use when:** comparing a mean, two means, paired changes, proportions, categorical variables, or three or more means.

**Do:** predeclare population, null/alternative, \(\alpha\), smallest important effect, design, assumptions, and missing-data rule; select one-sample z/t, Welch two-sample t, paired t, proportion test, chi-square association/goodness-of-fit, ANOVA, variance comparison, or runs test by data/design; calculate statistic/df/p-value and effect interval; inspect raw data, process order, and residuals.

**Stop when:** groups are confounded, independence/pairing is wrong, expected counts are inadequate, multiple testing is uncontrolled, or product acceptance is being delegated to statistical significance.

**Output:** hypothesis/design, exact equation/substitution, statistic/df/p-value, effect and interval, diagnostics, practical interpretation, and authority boundary. See [the comparisons playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/18-hypothesis-tests-and-comparisons.md).
