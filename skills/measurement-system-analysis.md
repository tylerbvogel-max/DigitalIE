# Measurement-system analysis

**Use when:** acceptance, comparison, SPC, capability, or root-cause inference depends on measurement discrimination.

**Do:** define decision, range, resolution, traceability, environment, appraisers, parts, repeats, and study design; calculate bias, variance components, \(\sigma_{GRR}\), total variation, percent study variation, ndc, and false-accept/reject implications as applicable.

**Stop when:** calibration is substituted for fitness, study design cannot separate components, sampled parts do not span operating variation, or a universal cutoff is used without decision risk.

**Output:** study design, equations/method/software, component estimates, interactions/diagnostics, decision risk, and fitness determination. See [the playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/09-measurement-system-analysis.md).
