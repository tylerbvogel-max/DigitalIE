# Statistical estimation

**Use when:** estimating a population mean/proportion, standard error, confidence interval, or estimator bias/precision.

**Do:** identify estimand and sampling design; distinguish SD from SE; calculate \(SE(\bar x)=s/\sqrt n\); use z only for genuinely known \(\sigma\), otherwise t for a mean; use Wilson/exact proportion intervals when simple normal approximation is weak; report estimate, interval, method, and practical threshold.

**Stop when:** observations are dependent, sample selection is unrepresentative, or the interval method is invalid for the data without an explicit alternative.

**Output:** estimand, estimator/bias basis, equation/substitution, estimate, standard error, interval, assumptions, and decision adequacy. See [the estimation playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/17-estimation-and-confidence-intervals.md).
