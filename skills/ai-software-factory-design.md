# Software factory design

**Use when:** repeated software demand should flow through reusable architecture and governed delivery mechanics.

**Do:** map demand-to-outcome flow; standardize repository, CI/CD, secure defaults, skills/tools, evals, review, deploy/rollback, telemetry, ownership, and learning; measure accepted lead time, defects, rework, reuse, cost, and value.

**Stop when:** the supposed product family is not repeatable or factory throughput would optimize output over business outcome.

**Output:** reference pipeline, golden repository, reusable capability catalog, gates, telemetry, and improvement backlog.
