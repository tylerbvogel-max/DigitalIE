# Manufacturing technology insertion

**Use when:** introducing or scaling new equipment, automation, software, AI, inspection, material, or process technology.

**Do:** begin with a measured operational problem; declare maturity and application boundary; map product, people, facility, data/security, supplier, qualification, configuration, and sustainment dependencies; progress through representative evidence gates; compare lifecycle value; define cutover, monitoring, degraded mode, rollback, and ownership.

**Stop when:** a pilot is not production-representative, controlled change/qualification is unresolved, failure recovery is absent, or the technology would make an unauthorized decision.

**Output:** staged evidence case, receipts, risks, qualification/change plan, lifecycle economics, cutover/rollback, measures, and gate decision. See [the playbook](../playbooks/aerospace-manufacturing/12-manufacturing-technology-insertion.md).
