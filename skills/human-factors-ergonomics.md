# Human factors and ergonomics

**Use when:** work content, station design, human-machine interaction, fatigue, error likelihood, or physical/cognitive demand may affect safety, quality, or flow.

**Do:** observe representative work and abnormal recovery; record posture, force, repetition, duration, reach, visibility, feedback, interruptions, environment, qualifications, and product mix; apply only a named current assessment method; separate system design from individual attribution; route controlled findings to qualified safety/medical/engineering authority.

**Stop when:** exposure limits, medical judgment, regulatory compliance, or immediate safety controls are implicated.

**Output:** evidence-linked task analysis, declared method, calculation receipt where applicable, uncertainty, design risks, alternatives, escalation, and trial plan. See [the playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/21-human-factors-ergonomics-and-work-measurement.md).
