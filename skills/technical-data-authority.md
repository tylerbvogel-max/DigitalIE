# Technical data authority

**Use when:** technical information is missing, stale, replicated, transformed, restricted, or disputed.

**Do:** identify system/source authority, identity, revision/status/effectivity, lineage, access, retention, validation, correction, archive, and availability need.

**Stop when:** access, classification, controlled-record correction, or source precedence requires an authorized owner.

**Output:** authority/lineage map, reconciliation gaps, availability risk, and owner decision.
