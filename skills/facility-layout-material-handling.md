# Facility layout and material handling

**Use when:** a cell, line, warehouse, receiving path, inspection area, or support function is being designed or materially rearranged.

**Do:** establish demand/mix scenarios; map from-to flow, loads, status, space, utilities, service, egress, environmental and segregation constraints; compare alternatives with named calculations; walk normal and abnormal scenarios; obtain facilities, safety, quality, maintenance, and operational review.

**Stop when:** site constraints or approvals are unknown, a move could compromise product/environmental control, or a numerical score hides a hard constraint.

**Output:** evidence-linked alternatives, receipts, scenario/sensitivity results, constraints, staged implementation, validation measures, rollback, and approval. See [the playbook](../playbooks/industrial-engineering/22-facility-layout-and-material-handling.md).
