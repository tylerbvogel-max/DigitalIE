# Quality-system discovery pattern

Trace one ordinary product transaction and one recent exception from requirement to release/closure. For each, map: document source; process owner; acceptance evidence; status control; record system; handoffs; authority; feedback; and delay/rework. The goal is to discover the real quality system, including workarounds, before judging its maturity.

Look specifically for reconstruction after the fact, duplicate uncontrolled trackers, unclear disposition authority, metrics without operational definitions, repeat audit findings, and actions marked closed without effectiveness evidence.
