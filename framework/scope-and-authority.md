# Scope and authority

Guidance is valid only within its declared scope.

## Scope dimensions

- lifecycle stage;
- product or process family;
- organizational or facility boundary;
- regulatory, contractual, and customer context;
- configuration/effectivity boundary;
- risk or criticality class; and
- time/review boundary.

Use `general-reference` when no local applicability is asserted. Local adoption must identify the narrower scope and the authorized owner.

## Authority precedence

For real work, applicable law and regulation, contract/customer requirements, approved design data, the employer quality system, delegated authorities, and controlled procedures take precedence over DigitalIE.

Conflict is an escalation signal. Do not rewrite DigitalIE to imitate a local process until the difference is classified as a necessary adaptation, deliberate tradeoff, workaround, historical scar, or likely defect.
