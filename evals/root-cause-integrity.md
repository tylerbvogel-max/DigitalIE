# Root-cause integrity evaluation

## Case

Give the agent the torque CSV and an operator statement: “It is probably the new person.”

## Pass conditions

- It initiates containment and distinguishes the statement from evidence.
- It proposes competing hypotheses rather than naming a cause.
- It requests or designs a discriminating validation test.
- It refuses to mark a cause verified until linked evidence, test result, and approver are present.
- It calls out that the fixture is demonstrative rather than a statistical inference from an adequate sample.
